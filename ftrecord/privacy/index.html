Privacy Policy

Effective September 14, 2026

Overview

FTRecord is designed to record and process media entirely on your Mac. The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Recording data

When you explicitly start a recording, FTRecord may process the screen, selected windows or regions, camera video, microphone audio, and system audio according to your settings. This content is processed locally and written to the folder you choose.

Permissions

FTRecord requests macOS permission only when a feature needs screen recording, system audio, camera, or microphone access. You can review or revoke these permissions in System Settings. Features requiring a revoked permission will stop working until access is restored.

Files and recovery

Recordings are saved locally, by default in Movies/FTRecord. A security-scoped bookmark may be stored on your Mac if you choose another folder. During recording, temporary recovery information may be stored in Application Support and is removed after successful completion, recovery, or discard.

Sharing

FTRecord opens the macOS share picker only when you choose Share. Any transfer is performed by the service you select and is governed by that service’s privacy terms. FTRecord does not upload recordings by itself.

Preferences and diagnostics

Recording settings, shortcut choices, first-run status, and login-item preference are stored locally. FTRecord has no accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking, cloud synchronization, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

Retention and deletion

Because no personal data is collected on a developer server, there is no server-side personal data to retain or delete. You control local recordings through FTRecord or Finder. Removing FTRecord and its associated app data removes locally stored preferences and recovery information.

Changes and contact

If FTRecord’s data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated before the changed functionality is released.

For privacy questions, visit the FTRecord support page or contact the developer through GitHub.
