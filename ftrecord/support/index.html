FTRecord Support

Requires macOS 15 or later on Apple silicon

Start a recording

Open FTRecord, choose Screen, Camera, or Audio in Settings, and select your video and audio options. Click Start Recording. For screen mode, drag to select a region, adjust the preparation controls if needed, then click the red record button.

Permissions

FTRecord may need Screen & System Audio, Camera, and Microphone permissions. Open FTRecord Settings > Permissions to see their status and jump to macOS System Settings. After changing screen recording access, quit and reopen FTRecord.

Pause and stop

Use the floating control bar, the menu bar icon, or your selected global shortcuts to pause, resume, or stop. A red border and visible control bar indicate an active screen recording.

Recordings and storage

Recordings are saved to Movies/FTRecord by default. Choose another folder in FTRecord Settings > Recording. The media library lets you preview, rename, reveal, share, trim, or move a recording to the Trash.

Recover an interrupted recording

Open Recovery in the sidebar. FTRecord lists playable fragments left by interrupted sessions. Choose Recover to move a playable file to its intended folder, or Discard to remove it.

Contact

For support, bug reports, or feedback, contact the developer through GitHub. Include your macOS and FTRecord versions, recording mode, permission status, and steps to reproduce. Do not include recordings, passwords, or sensitive information.
